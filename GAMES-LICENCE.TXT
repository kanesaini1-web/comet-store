Games for Comet: Brick Breaker, Block Drop, Sky Defenders, Rock Blaster and Floppy.

They are the "classics" of raylib-games (https://github.com/raysan5/raylib-games) by
Ramon Santamaria (@raysan5), built with raylib 5.5 (https://www.raylib.com).

ALTERED VERSIONS (as the zlib licence below asks to be said plainly): only the window
titles were changed, from "classic game: arkanoid / tetris / space invaders /
asteroids / floppy" to the names above. The games are otherwise unchanged.
These are not the original arcade games and are not connected with their makers;
their names belong to their owners.

raylib also contains: GLFW (zlib/libpng licence, below), and single-file libraries
that are public domain or MIT-licensed (stb, miniaudio, dr_libs, cgltf, qoi, qoa,
par_shapes, tinyobj_loader, msf_gif, sdefl/sinfl, m3d, vox_loader, jar_xm/jar_mod,
glad, dirent).
